2025–2026

MSc Thesis Research

Graphene Saturable Absorption & Optical Pulse Shaping · University of Ioannina

Explore the research
  • Adapted a Fortran carrier-dynamics model from picosecond to femtosecond excitation, accounting for faster dynamics and broader pulse bandwidth.
  • Coupled nonequilibrium carrier dynamics to the time-dependent optical response of multilayer photonic structures.
  • Developed a Python workflow to automate, manage, post-process and visualize more than 30,000 simulations.
  • Compared predictions with nonlinear-transmission measurements to assess model limitations and refine physical assumptions.
  • Optimized asymmetric graphene-loaded Fabry–Pérot cavities, predicting absorption enhancement from 2.3% to nearly 100%.
  • Numerically demonstrated passive all-optical pulse shaping from a single-peaked input into reflected profiles with two or three peaks.
  • Implemented a transfer-matrix model for graphene third-harmonic generation and a COMSOL benchmark model.
